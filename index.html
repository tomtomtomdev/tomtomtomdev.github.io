About

I'm a senior iOS engineer specialising in Swift, SwiftUI, and modern iOS architecture — MVVM and Clean Architecture — with a strong focus on performance, maintainability, and user experience. I care about what happens after the app ships: crash rates, screen load times, memory under load.

I started on UIKit and Objective-C, and I've been Swift-first ever since. On personal projects I've adopted Swift 6 strict concurrency end to end; at work I weigh changes like that carefully, because a regression in a trading app means real people can't complete their transactions.

I enjoy working in cross-functional teams with product, design, and backend, and solving hard product and performance problems. I'm open to international opportunities and relocation to Sweden or elsewhere in Europe.

Side project

StockLite TRADING APP · ZERO DEPENDENCIES

SwiftUI · Swift Charts · SwiftData · AsyncStream · Swift Testing · Clean Architecture

Built entirely on Apple's own SDK — no CocoaPods, no third-party libraries. The constraint was deliberate: understand how far the platform goes before reaching for abstractions.

Tested, not hoped

56 Swift Testing tests covering use cases, view models, repositories, pricing rules, and Keychain — using Swift Testing, not XCTest. Price-step rules match the real Indonesian exchange.

Concurrency design

@Observable with DI via .environment(), a simulated live WebSocket feed on AsyncStream — no false Sendable conformances, no nonisolated(unsafe) escape hatches. Swift 6 strict concurrency throughout.

Built into it

Price charts built with Swift Charts · a buy/sell order flow with input validation · Keychain-backed sessions.
